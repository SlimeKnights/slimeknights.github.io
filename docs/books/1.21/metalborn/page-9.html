Satchels

Satchels can be opened by interacting while held, or by right clicking from the inventory. Opening grants access to their slots plus the metalmind inventory, making them ideal for swapping metalminds.

Most satchels hold 9 items, though pewter holds up to 18. Steel satchels are immune to damage when dropped. Nicrosil satchels remain in the inventory on death.
