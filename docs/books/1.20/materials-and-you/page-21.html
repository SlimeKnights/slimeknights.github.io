Dagger

The dagger is a quick weapon with low damage. It can be used to attack from either hand for double the daggers.

In addition to combat, daggers are effective at mining many plants and glass blocks.

Properties:

  • Low Damage

  • High Speed

  • Able to use in off-hand

  • Stacks to 2 when crafting and modifying

Flint and Brick

Flint and Brick is an affordable tool effective for starting fires.
Unlike most other tools, it is crafted in a crafting station or crafting table.

Properties:

  • Creates fires

  • Starts with fiery 1

  • Repaired using seared or scorched bricks
