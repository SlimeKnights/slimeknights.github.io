Mattock

The Mattock is a versatile tool. It is effective on logs and dirt.
It can create the farm of your dreams.

Properties:

  • Tills dirt

  • Effective on dirt and logs

Pickadze

Proper name for this tool would be a pick mattock, but people like the familiar.
It can create paths to glory.

Properties:

  • Creates paths

  • Mines stone blocks and similar

  • Breaks soft dirts and gravel

  • Harvest level limited to wood
