Recycling Tools

Change your mind about a tool? You can use some venom to disassemble it in the part builder to salvage one of the tool parts. You can potentially salvage a second part, with a higher chance on repaired tools making this the more economical way to swap parts. Daggers require stacking to 2 in order to recycle.
