Bulk Quiver

Putting a quiver on your back is overrated.

Effects:

  • Allows your bow to store arrows

  • Access by sneaking and attacking

  • Multiple levels, each level increases storage size

  • Requires 1 ability slot per level

Channeling

Like Mjolnir!

Effects:

  • Adds a chance for lighting to strike projectiles based on weather

  • Single level

  • Requires 1 ability slot

  • Requires throwing, fishing, or a ballista bow
