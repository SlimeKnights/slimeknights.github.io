Features

  • Gives +3 nuggets or +1 gem per ore (by default)

  • Mass melting capabilities

  • Can hold multiple metals

  • Extendable capacity

  • Able to alloy

  • Fuel efficiency depends on size

  • Able to melt mobs to get their precious materials

  • Ideal for smelting lots of materials at once

Smeltery Controller

Using your melter, pour four ingots worth of copper on seared bricks to get your smeltery controller.
