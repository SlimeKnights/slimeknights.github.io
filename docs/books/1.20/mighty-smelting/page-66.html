Slimeball

The classical weapon of a wizard. Well, at least it's close enough.

Effects:

  • Interact to fire slimeballs as projectiles

  • Pulls ammo from inventory

  • Different effects on different slimeballs

  • Single level

  • Requires 1 ability

Sliver

Outsmart your opponents with surprising damage types!

Effects:

  • Each level adds 3 toggleable slimeball slots

  • Switch slots on interact, configure with worktable

  • Requires 1 ability slot per level

  • Requires Slimeball
