Ranged Abilities

Abilities to become the best archer you can be

These abilities allow your bow to do new and cool whacky things. Perhaps you will shoot crystals, or more than one arrow. Only time will tell.

Ballista

Regular arrows not doing it for you? Try ballistic ammo! Fire only the highest quality melee weapons.

Effects:

  • Allows your bow to fire melee weapons

  • Melee weapon must be in other hand or quiver

  • Single level

  • Requires 1 ability slot
