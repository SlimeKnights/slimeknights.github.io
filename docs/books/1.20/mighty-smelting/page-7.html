Seared Tanks

Tanks are good for storing molten fluids or fuels. If you want to melt metals in your smeltery you'll need one! However, they cannot be placed in the smeltery floor.

Tanks can be filled from buckets, potions, and can also be poured into from faucets. They can also be used to cast without a smeltery. Pistons break them, and dispensers can place them.

Seared Blocks

These blocks can be used for any part of the smeltery, as floors or walls. They are also useful for decorating your base.
