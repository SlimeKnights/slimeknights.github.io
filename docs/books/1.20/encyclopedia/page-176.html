Molten Queen's Slime

Sometimes you just want to blow up your enemies without blowing up your world.

Entity Effects:

  • Power 1 explosion, +1 power per level

  • Up to 3 Explosion Damage per level

Block Effects:

  • Explosion ignores blocks

  • Explosion causes fires

Molten Knightslime

All the boom with none of the consequences.

Entity Effects:

  • Power 1 explosion, +1 power per level

  • Up to 4 Explosion Damage per level

  • No explosion knockback

Block Effects:

  • Explosion ignores blocks
