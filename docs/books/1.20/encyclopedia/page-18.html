Battlesign

The battlesign is a cross between a shield and a sword, found in bastions, from bartering, or in the hands of piglins of all varieties.

Properties:

  • +2 Attack Damage

  • 1.4 Attack Speed

  • Blocks 50 damage

  • 120° block angle

  • 20% block speed

  • 3 Defense Slots

  • 1 Ability Slot

Melting Pan

The melting pan is a staff that mines meltable blocks, producing an extra 6 nuggets per ore (as opposed to the smeltery's extra 3 nuggets). Expanders work like a vein hammer.

Found from zombies, dungeons, igloos, and gifted from armorsmiths after a raid.

Properties:

  • 6 Mining Speed

  • Stone Harvest Tier

  • 10% Knockback Resistance

  • Melting+

  • 1 Defense Slot

  • 1 Upgrade Slot

  • 2 Ability Slots
