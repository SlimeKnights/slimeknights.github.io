Springing

Charge and release to launch yourself in the targeted direction for 1 durability.

Effect strength is based on half of power times velocity. Boosted by knockback multiplied by armor knockback such as ricochet and knockback resistance. Total effect halved by padded. Quick charge reduces charge time.

Effects:

  • Maximum of 1 level

  • Requires 1 ability slot

Stripping

Allows the tool to strip wood, similarly to hand or broad axes. Tools will use their normal mining area for stripping wood.

Triggers before the tool's native tilling or pathing if present.

Effects:

  • Maximum of 1 level

  • Requires 1 ability slot
