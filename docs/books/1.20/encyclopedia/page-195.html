Fiery Liquid

You might think this is a fantastic deal, so many extra ingots! But remember, fiery is really hard to get.

Block Effects:

  • Melts block into liquid

Molten Enderium

Molten enderium is ideal as a weapon, keeping enemies from teleporting as you fight.

Entity Effects:

  • 4.0 Magic Damage per level

  • 10 seconds Enderference per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 10 seconds Enderference per level
