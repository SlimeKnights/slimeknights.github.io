Molten Zinc

Molten zinc is probably not the best choice to boost your speed, perhaps the speed will throw off an opponent.

Entity Effects:

  • 2.0 Fire Damage per level

  • 10 seconds Speed per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 10 seconds Speed per level

Molten Brass

Molten brass helps you resist the heat, and the second sip is free if you time it right.

Entity Effects:

  • 3.0 Fire Damage per level

  • 8 seconds Fire Resistance per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 8 seconds Fire Resistance per level
