Thorns

Has a 15% chance per level to apply 1 to 4 damage to the attacker. Multiple pieces will stack the effect.

Shields are twice as effective but only work when blocking.

Effects:

  • Maximum of 3 levels

  • Requires 1 upgrade slot per level

Item Frame

Adds 3 slots per level to the helmet which will render on the UI when the helmet is worn.

Helmet inventory can be opened by interacting with it while holding.

Effects:

  • Requires 1 upgrade slot per level
