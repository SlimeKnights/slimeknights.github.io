Prismarine

Arrow Head

Fins

Lure

Lets the ammo move freely underwater and grants +1 projectile damage per level to underwater targets. Fishing rods instead gain a level of lure.

Redstone

Arrow Head

Supercharged

Grants +10% velocity per level to arrows, and +20% velocity per level to thrown projectiles.
