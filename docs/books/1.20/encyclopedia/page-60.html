Gunpowder

Arrow Head

Explosive

Causes the arrow to explode at a radius of 1 plus 1 per level. Explosion damage is based on projectile power.

Prismarine

Arrow Head

Fins

Lure

Lets the ammo move freely underwater and grants +1 projectile damage per level to underwater targets. Fishing rods instead gain a level of lure.
