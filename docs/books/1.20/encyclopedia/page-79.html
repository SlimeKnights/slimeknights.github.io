Plate Chestplate

Plate Chestplate is a defensive chestplate, with high protection, and low ability slots and high defense slots.

Properties:

  • Material controlled stats
  • 40% Unarmed Attack
  • 2 Upgrade Slot
  • 3 Defense Slots
  • 2 Material Traits
