Ender Pearl

Arrow Head

Enderporting

Causes you to teleport to the location the projectile lands, destroying it. If it hits a monster, swaps places.

Magnetite

Arrow Head

Attractive

Attracts mobs within a radius of 3 blocks per level towards the place the projectile hits.
