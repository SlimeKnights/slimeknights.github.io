Depth Strider

Reduces the penalty of moving underwater by 33% per level. At level 3, there is no underwater movement penalty.

Effects:

  • Maximum of 3 levels
  • Requires 1 upgrade slot per level

Feather Falling

Grants +25% resistance to fall damage per level, up to a maximum of +50% at 2 levels.

Effects:

  • Can apply levels incrementally
  • Maximum of 2 levels
  • Requires 1 upgrade slot per level
