Ichor

Ichor is a reverse gravity fluid, helping you also reverse gravity. Surviving the trip back down is on you.

Entity Effects:

  • +7 seconds Slow Falling per level

  • +5 seconds Slowness per level

Block Effects:

  • Places held block

Enderslime

Teleporting your problems to a random location is a method of dealing with them.

Entity Effects:

  • +7 Seconds Returning per level

  • Teleports target if no current Returning

Block Effects:

  • Replaces hit block with held block
