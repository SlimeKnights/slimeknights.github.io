Blazing Bone

Arrow Shaft

Conductive

Applies conductive to the target, making them take 200% fire damage for the next 5 to 10 seconds.

Blazewood

Arrow Shaft

Spectral

Causes the target to glow for 10 seconds. If it hits a block, places a permanent light source at the cost of the projectile.
