Plate Shield

The plate shield is a highly defensive shield, granting high blocking power at the cost of speed while blocking.

Properties:

  • Full defense stats
  • Blocks 100 damage
  • 180° block angle
  • 20% block speed
  • 2 Upgrade Slot
  • 3 Defense Slots
  • 2 Material Traits

Slimeskull

The slimeskull is a helmet that copies abilities from monsters. It is created from pouring slime on a mob head, with the head type determining the effects.

Properties:

  • Low defense
  • 3 Upgrade Slots
  • 0 Defense Slots
  • 2 Ability Slots
  • Slime & Skull Traits
  • Mob Disguise
