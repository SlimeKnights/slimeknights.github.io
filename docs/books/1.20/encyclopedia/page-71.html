Nahuatl

Arrow Shaft

Lacerating

Causes 1 damage per second over time for 2 seconds per level. Consecutive hits add instead of replacing the time.

Blazing Bone

Arrow Shaft

Conductive

Applies conductive to the target, making them take 200% fire damage for the next 5 to 10 seconds.
