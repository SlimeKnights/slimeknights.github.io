Molten Tin

They say carrots are good for your eyes, but molten tin is better.

Entity Effects:

  • 2.0 Fire Damage per level

  • 8 seconds Night Vision per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 8 seconds Night Vision per level

Molten Pewter

Molten pewter is probably lower risk than venom to boost your strength, but not by much.

Entity Effects:

  • 3.0 Fire Damage per level

  • 7 seconds Strength per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 7 seconds Strength per level
