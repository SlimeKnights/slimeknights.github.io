Traveler's Goggles

Traveler's Goggles are a balanced helmet, with low protection, and average upgrade and defense slots.

Properties:

  • 75% Durability Modifier
  • 75% Plating Stats
  • 2 Upgrade Slots
  • 2 Defense Slots
  • 1 Ability Slot
  • 2 Material Traits
  • Insulated

Traveler's Vest

Traveler's Vest is a balanced chestplate, with medium protection, and average upgrade and defense slots.

Properties:

  • 75% Durability Modifier
  • 75% Plating Stats
  • 2 Upgrade Slots
  • 2 Defense Slots
  • 1 Ability Slot
  • 2 Material Traits
  • Insulated
