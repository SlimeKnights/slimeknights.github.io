Excavator

The Excavator is a broad digging tool, effective on dirt, gravel, and sand in a 3x3 range.

Expanders alternate between increasing width and height.

Properties:

  • +1.5 Attack Damage
  • 120% Attack Multiplier
  • 1.0 Attack Speed
  • 30% Mining Multiplier
  • 375% Durability Multiplier
  • Knockback II
  • Makes path

Scythe

The Scythe is a broad reaping tool, effective on plants or monsters in a 3x3x3 range.

Expanders alternate between increasing width/height and depth, while silky grants AOE shearing.

Properties:

  • +1 Attack Damage
  • 0.7 Attack Speed
  • 45% Mining Multiplier
  • 250% Durability Multiplier
  • AOE Attack
  • Tills dirt
  • Harvest plants
