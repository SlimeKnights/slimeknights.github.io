Molten Netherite

Molten netherite is the highest base damage, for when you cannot wait for your target to bleed out.

Entity Effects:

  • 5.0 Damage per level

  • 5 seconds Blindness per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 5 seconds Blindness per level

Molten Knightmetal

Ironically, Knightmetal is most suited to killing those from who it comes.

Entity Effects:

  • 3.0 Piercing Damage per level

  • 5 seconds Pierced II per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 5 seconds Pierced II per level
