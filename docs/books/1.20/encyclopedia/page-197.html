Molten Refined Obsidian

Molten refined obsidian maximizes damage against armored targets.

Entity Effects:

  • 3.0 Piercing Damage per level

  • 2 seconds Bleeding per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 2 seconds Bleeding per level

Molten Signalum

Molten signalum is great for inverted explosive combat with minimal environmental damage.

Entity Effects:

  • Power 1 explosion, +1 power per level

  • Up to 2 Explosion Damage per level

  • Inverted explosion knockback

Block Effects:

  • Explosion ignores blocks
