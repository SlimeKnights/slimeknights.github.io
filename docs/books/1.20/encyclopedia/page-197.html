Magic Protection

Grants +10% resistance against magic damage, such as from poison or thorns. Caps at 80% across the whole set, or 8 levels.

Reduces the duration of negative potions by 5% per level of the highest level piece.

Effects:

  • Can apply levels incrementally
  • Requires 1 defense slot per level

Melee Protection

Grants +8% resistance against melee damage, such as from zombies and swords. Caps at 80% across the whole set, or 10 levels.

Increases movement speed when using items by 5% per level of the highest level piece.

Effects:

  • Can apply levels incrementally
  • Requires 1 defense slot per level
