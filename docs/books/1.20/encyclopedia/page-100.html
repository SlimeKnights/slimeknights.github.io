Slimeshell

The slimeshell improves storage and grants useful unique bonuses. They are created by pouring slime on a shell.

Properties:

  • Low defense

  • 3 Upgrade Slots

  • 0 Defense Slots

  • 2 Ability Slots

  • Slime & Shell Traits

  • Shell Storage

Slimeboots

Slime Boots are high mobility boots with minimal protection. They can be created by pouring slime on laces.

Properties:

  • Low defense

  • 3 Upgrade Slots

  • 0 Defense Slots

  • 2 Ability Slots

  • Slime & Laces Traits

  • Bouncy
