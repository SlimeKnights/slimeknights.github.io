Molten Lumium

Molten lumium is either used to keep your eye on the target or make a quick but obvious escape.

Entity Effects:

  • 4.0 Magic Damage per level

  • 5 seconds Glowing per level

  • 5 seconds Speed per level

  • 5 seconds Jump Boost per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 5 seconds Glowing per level and 5 seconds Speed per level and 5 seconds Jump Boost per level

Molten Refined Glowstone

Molten refined glowstone is great for tracking targets without sacrificing much damage.

Entity Effects:

  • 3.0 Magic Damage per level

  • 10 seconds Glowing per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 10 seconds Glowing per level
