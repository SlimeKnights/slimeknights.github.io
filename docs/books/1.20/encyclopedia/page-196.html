Molten Signalum

Molten signalum is great for inverted explosive combat with minimal environmental damage.

Entity Effects:

  • Power 1 explosion, +1 power per level

  • Up to 2 Explosion Damage per level

  • Inverted explosion knockback

Block Effects:

  • Explosion ignores blocks

Molten Steeleaf

Useful both for you and your target to maximize gained experience.

Entity Effects:

  • 2.0 Magic Damage per level

  • 5 seconds Experienced per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 5 seconds Experienced per level
