Gilded

Trades 1 ability slot for 2 upgrade slots.

Effects:

  • No max level

  • Requires 1 ability slot per level

Lucky

Increases drop rates from blocks (on harvest tools) monsters (on melee weapons), and when fishing (on fishing rods). Luck requires a different recipe for each level, applied in order.

Effects:

  • Maximum of 3 levels

  • First level requires 1 ability slot

  • Second and third levels are slotless
