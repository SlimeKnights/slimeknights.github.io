Molten Nickel

Molten nickel lets you harm your enemies and reduce the risk of retaliation, what's not to love?

Entity Effects:

  • 2.0 Fire Damage per level

  • 7 seconds Weakness per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 7 seconds Weakness per level

Molten Invar

Molten invar may be useful if you are trying to eat that powerful cake, but otherwise is an odd fluid.

Entity Effects:

  • 3.0 Fire Damage per level

  • 10 seconds Hunger per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 10 seconds Hunger per level
