Plate Boots

Plate Boots are defensive boots, with high protection, and low ability slots and high defense slots.

Properties:

  • Full defense stats

  • 2 Upgrade Slot

  • 3 Defense Slots

  • 2 Material Traits

Plate Shield

The plate shield is a highly defensive shield, granting high blocking power at the cost of speed while blocking.

Properties:

  • Full defense stats

  • Blocks 100 damage

  • 180° block angle

  • 20% block speed

  • 2 Upgrade Slot

  • 3 Defense Slots

  • 2 Material Traits
