Piglin Skull

Skull

Repair Value: 41

Chrysophilite

Piglin Disguise

Causes some gold ores to drop extra gold nuggets, and increases drop chance of gold armor by 4%. Nuggets and armor drops are further boosted if trimmed and by each piece of golden armor worn.

Piglin Brute Skull

Skull

Repair Value: 58

Gold Guard

Piglin Brute Disguise

Boosts health by 2 hearts, plus an additional 2 hearts when trimmed and per piece of golden armor.
