Molten Aluminum

Molten aluminium is like milk, but aggressive. Ideal for combat when your opponents have too many effects.

Entity Effects:

  • 2.0 Magic Damage per level

  • Cures most status effects

Molten Duralumin

Disconnects you from the world.

Entity Effects:

  • 3.0 Magic Damage per level

  • 5 seconds Invisibility per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 5 seconds Invisibility per level
