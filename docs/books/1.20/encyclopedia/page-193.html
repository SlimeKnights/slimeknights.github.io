Molten Hepatizon

Cold is a rare damage source, but can be neutralized by warm clothes. The withering may help in that case.

Entity Effects:

  • 4.0 Cold Damage per level

  • 8 seconds Wither per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 8 seconds Wither per level

Molten Manyullyn

Molten manyullyn has the most damage potential between flat damage and damage over time.

Entity Effects:

  • 4.0 Fire Damage per level

  • 3 seconds Bleeding per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 3 seconds Bleeding per level
