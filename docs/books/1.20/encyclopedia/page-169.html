Molten Amethyst

Molten amethyst is the cheapest mining fluid, but its not very strong.

Block Effects:

  • Mines up to hardness 3.0 per level

Molten Quartz

Molten quartz is a modest mining fluid, not too expensive and not too strong.

Block Effects:

  • Mines up to hardness 5.0 per level
