Venom

Venom pairs very well with venombone, the hint is in the name.

Entity Effects:

  • +5 seconds Poison per level

  • +10 seconds Strength per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 5 seconds Poison per level and 10 seconds Strength per level

Magma

Ever wish you were a ghast? This gets you partway there.

Entity Effects:

  • Power 0.5 explosion +0.5 per level

  • Up to 2 Explosion Damage per level

Block Effects:

  • Explosion breaks blocks

  • Explosion causes fires
