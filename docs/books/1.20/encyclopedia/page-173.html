Step Up

Increases the height the player can step by 0.5 blocks per level.

Effects:

  • Maximum of 2 levels
  • Requires 1 upgrade slot per level

Swift Sneak

Reduces the sneaking movement speed penalty by 15% per level. At level 5, you may sprint while sneaking.

Effects:

  • Maximum of 5 levels
  • Requires 1 upgrade slot per level
