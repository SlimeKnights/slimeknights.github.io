Molten Cobalt

Inhaling the lingering cloud is the safest way to use molten cobalt for haste, or just use it as a weapon.

Entity Effects:

  • 3.0 Fire Damage per level

  • 7 seconds Haste per level

  • 7 seconds Speed per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 7 seconds Haste per level and 7 seconds Speed per level

Molten Hepatizon

Is molten hepatizon best for defense, or combat? Either is a risk reward setup.

Entity Effects:

  • 4.0 Cold Damage per level

  • 8 seconds Wither per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 8 seconds Wither per level
