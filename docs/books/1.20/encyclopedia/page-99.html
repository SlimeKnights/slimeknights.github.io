Slimeskull

The slimeskull is a helmet that copies abilities from monsters. It is created from pouring slime on a mob head, with the head type determining the effects.

Properties:

  • Low defense

  • 3 Upgrade Slots

  • 0 Defense Slots

  • 2 Ability Slots

  • Slime & Skull Traits

  • Mob Disguise

Slimecage

The slimecage is the ultimate unarmed combat weapon with its ribcage traits boosting unarmed attacks. Created by pouring slime on a ribcage.

Properties:

  • Low defense

  • 75% Unarmed Attack

  • 3 Upgrade Slots

  • 0 Defense Slots

  • 2 Ability Slots

  • Slime & 2 Ribcage Traits

  • Reach
