Meat Soup

Don't ask what is in the meat soup; just enjoy the flavor as you slurp this down.

Entity Effects:

  • Restores 2 hunger per level

Molten Clay

Great for quickly building a wall, or just solidifying clay without a cast.

Entity Effects:

  • 2.0 Damage per level

  • Drops Brick

Block Effects:

  • Places Bricks
