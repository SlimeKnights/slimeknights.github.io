Molten Gold

Molten gold first hurts you, then it heals you back more. Is it worth it? Up to you.

Entity Effects:

  • 2.0 Magic Damage per level

  • 6 seconds Regeneration II per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 6 seconds Regeneration II per level

Molten Rose Gold

Molten rose gold lets you lose a small amount of health for a temporary edge.

Entity Effects:

  • 3.0 Magic Damage per level

  • 15 seconds Absorption II per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 15 seconds Absorption II per level
