Mushroom Stew

Mushroom stew is a very affordable soup option for slurping on the go.

Entity Effects:

  • Restores 1 hunger per level

Rabbit Stew

Rabbit stew is the best liquid food available, ideal for slurping.

Entity Effects:

  • Restores 2 hunger per level
