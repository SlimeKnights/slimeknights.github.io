Glowstone

Arrow Head

Spectral

Causes the target to glow for 10 seconds per level. If it hits a block, places a permanent light source at the cost of the projectile.

Arrow Head

Rebound

Bounce

Projectile will bounce upon hitting a block up to 1 time, plus 2 per level. Increases power by 0.5 per bounce per level before hitting the target.
