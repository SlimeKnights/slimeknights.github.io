Steeleaf

Arrow Head

Looting

Increases loot drops of targets killed with the projectile. Stacks with luck and looting from bows.

Ice

Arrow Head

Freezing

Freezes the target for 4 seconds (plus 4 seconds per level), causing slowness and damage over time.
