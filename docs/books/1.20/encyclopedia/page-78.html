Traveler's Shield

Traveler's Shield is a lightweight shield with limited blocking potential but quick movement while blocking.

Properties:

  • 200 Durability
  • Blocks 10 damage
  • 90° block angle
  • 80% block speed
  • 2 Upgrade Slots
  • 2 Defense Slots
  • 1 Ability Slot
  • 2 Material Traits

Plate Helmet

Plate Helmet is a defensive helmet, with high protection, and low ability slots and high defense slots.

Properties:

  • Material controlled stats
  • 2 Upgrade Slot
  • 3 Defense Slots
  • 2 Material Traits
