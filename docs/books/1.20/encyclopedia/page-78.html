Traveler's Boots

Traveler's Boots are balanced boots, with low protection, and average upgrade and defense slots.

Properties:

  • 75% Durability Modifier
  • 75% Plating Stats
  • 2 Upgrade Slots
  • 2 Defense Slots
  • 1 Ability Slot
  • 2 Material Traits
  • Insulated
  • Snow Step

Traveler's Shield

Traveler's Shield is a lightweight shield with limited blocking potential but quick movement while blocking.

Properties:

  • 200 Durability
  • Blocks 10 damage
  • 90° block angle
  • 80% block speed
  • 2 Upgrade Slots
  • 2 Defense Slots
  • 1 Ability Slot
  • 2 Material Traits
