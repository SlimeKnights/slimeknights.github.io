Slimeboots

Slime Boots are high mobility boots with minimal protection. They can be created by pouring slime on laces.

Properties:

  • Low defense
  • 3 Upgrade Slots
  • 0 Defense Slots
  • 2 Ability Slots
  • Slime & Laces Traits
  • Bouncy

Slimelytra

The slimelytra is the latest in flight technology. Created by pouring slime on an elytra, these wings can also be repaired with phantom membranes in addition to their slime type.

Properties:

  • Low defense
  • 50% Unarmed Attack
  • +222 Durability
  • 4 Upgrade Slots
  • 0 Defense Slots
  • 1 Ability Slot
  • Slime Trait
  • Wings
