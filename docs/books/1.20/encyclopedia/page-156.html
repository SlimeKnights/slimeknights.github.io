Leather Laces

Laces

Repair Value: 32

Rugged

Allows walking on powder snow and prevents uncomfortable damage from many blocks. When trimmed, prevents damage from thorns.

Vine Laces

Laces

Repair Value: 25

Solar Powered

Tool has a 5% chance per sunlight level to resist damage, up to a 75% chance in full sunlight. Trim increases the chance to 6% per sunlight for a max of 90%.
