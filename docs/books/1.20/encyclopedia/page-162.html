Blazing Blood

Blazing blood is an acceptable fluid for combat, but it really shines as a light source.

Entity Effects:

  • 2.0 Fire Damage per level

  • +2 seconds fire per level

  • +5 seconds Glowing per level

Block Effects:

  • Places Glow

Powder Snow

Powder snow is a useful fluid for nether combat, dealing bonus damage to blazing mobs.

Entity Effects:

  • 2.0 Cold Damage per level

  • +4 seconds freeze per level

Block Effects:

  • Places Snow
