Twisting Vine Laces

Laces

Repair Value: 29

Entwined

Increases movement speed by 15% per level, but deals 2 damage per level when unequipped.

Weeping Vine Laces

Laces

Repair Value: 30

Soulspeed

Grants +40.5% speed on soul sand, plus an additional 10.5% added for each additional level. Reduces the slowdown from walking on soulsand.
