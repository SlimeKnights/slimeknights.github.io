Nahuatl

Shield Core

Lacerating

Has a 25% chance per level (50% if blocking) of doing up to 5 damage over time.

Necronium

Shield Core

Decay

Has a 50% chance when hit (or 100% chance if blocking) to wither the attacker for 6 seconds, plus a 10% chance to wither you.
