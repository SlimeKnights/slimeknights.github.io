Plate Chestplate

Plate Chestplate is a defensive chestplate, with high protection, and low ability slots and high defense slots.

Properties:

  • Full defense stats

  • 50% Unarmed Attack

  • 2 Upgrade Slot

  • 3 Defense Slots

  • 2 Material Traits

Plate Leggings

Plate Leggings are defensive leggings, with high protection, and low ability slots and high defense slots.

Properties:

  • Full defense stats

  • 2 Upgrade Slot

  • 3 Defense Slots

  • 2 Material Traits
