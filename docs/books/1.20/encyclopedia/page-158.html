Weeping Vine Laces

Laces

Repair Value: 30

Soulspeed

Grants +40.5% speed on soul sand, plus an additional 10.5% added for each additional level. Reduces the slowdown from walking on soulsand.

Cheese Laces

Laces

Repair Value: 37

Savory

Grants a 15% chance per level of eating the armor (consuming some durability) for 1 hunger per level at 0.4 saturation and to removes a random effect.
