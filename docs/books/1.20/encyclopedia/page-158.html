Armor Pierce

Arrows cancel out 1 point of armor per level which lasts for 4 seconds after hit.

Effects:

  • Can apply levels incrementally
  • Maximum of 3 levels
  • Requires 1 upgrade slot per level

Arrow Pierce

Allows arrows to keep moving after damaging the first target, potentially hitting multiple targets. Can pass through one entity per level.

Effects:

  • Maximum of 4 levels
  • Requires 1 upgrade slot per level
