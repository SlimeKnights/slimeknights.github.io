Earthslime Staff

Earthslime Staff is a high defense and accuracy staff variant.

Properties:

  • 800 Durability

  • 1 Projectile Power

  • 0.9 Accuracy

  • 35 Max Blocking

  • 140 Block Angle

  • 2 Upgrade Slots

  • 3 Defense Slots

  • 2 Ability Slots

Ichor Staff

  • 1.2 Velocity

  • 0.75 Draw Speed

  • 2 Upgrade Slots

  • 3 Ability Slots
