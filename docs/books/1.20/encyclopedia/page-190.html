Molten Lead

Molten lead gives your attacks a little extra boost, if you are willing to wait.

Entity Effects:

  • 2.0 Fire Damage per level

  • 5 seconds Poison per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 5 seconds Poison per level

Molten Platinum

Not sure the applications of molten platinum slowing your enemies' mining speed, but you can probably figure one out.

Entity Effects:

  • 2.0 Fire Damage per level

  • 10 seconds Mining Fatigue per level

Block Effects:

  • Lingering cloud with 10 seconds Mining Fatigue per level
