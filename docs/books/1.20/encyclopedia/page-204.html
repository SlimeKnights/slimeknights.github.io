Flinging

Charge and release to launch yourself away from the targeted block for 1 durability.

Effect strength is based on half of power times velocity. Boosted by 25% per knockback level, multiplied by armor knockback such as ricochet and knockback resistance. Total effect is halved by padded. Quick charge reduces charge time.

Effects:

  • Maximum of 1 level

  • Requires 1 ability slot

Glowing

Places glows at the cost of 5 durability, which give off light until broken. Glows can be placed on interaction or by launching a projectile, which will be consumed on hit.

Can also be used in place of blocks for exchanging or fluid effects, which will place glows when this is in the offhand or the offhand is empty.

Effects:

  • Maximum of 1 level

  • Requires 1 ability slot
