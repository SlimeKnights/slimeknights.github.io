Armor Trim

Combine armor trim templates with a trim material to add +1 defense slot and a colored variant of a unique texture to the armor.

On the slimesuit, grants an extra copy of the mob part trait instead of a defense slot.

Effects:

  • Swappable

  • Slotless

Draconic

Grants +1 ability slot.

Effects:

  • Maximum of 1 level

  • Slotless
