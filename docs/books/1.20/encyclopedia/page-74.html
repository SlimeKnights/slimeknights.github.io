Wool

Arrow Head

Soft

Multiplies the projectile damage by 0. Note damage may still be increased by bow power, and it may still aggravate mobs.

Amethyst

Arrow Head

Crystalbound

Grants +0.75 projectile damage per level, but restricts the angles the projectile may be thrown at. Stacks with amethyst bronze bows to further restrict angles.
