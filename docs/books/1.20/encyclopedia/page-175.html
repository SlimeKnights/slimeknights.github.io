Molten Slimesteel

Slimesteel is ideal for anyone wishing to fly; as long as you found a way to deal with the landing.

Entity Effects:

  • Power 1 explosion, +0.5 power per level

  • 0 Explosion Damage

  • Extra explosion knockback, scaling with level

Block Effects:

  • Explosion breaks blocks

Molten Cinderslime

Cinderslime flows the wrong way, and its explosion goes the wrong way.

Entity Effects:

  • Power 1 explosion, +1 power per level

  • Up to 3 Explosion Damage per level

  • Inverted explosion knockback

Block Effects:

  • Explosion breaks blocks
