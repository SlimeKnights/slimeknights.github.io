Harvest Abilities

Harvest abilities are most useful on tools that break blocks, such as pickaxes or shovels.

Autosmelt

Causes items dropped from mined blocks to be smelted, as if in a furnace.

Effects:

  • Maximum of 1 level

  • Requires 1 ability slot
