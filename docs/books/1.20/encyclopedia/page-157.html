Slimy Vine Laces

Laces

Repair Value: 50

Leaping

Grants +0.5 blocks jump height at first level, and +1 at second level. Increases the safe fall distance by 1 block per level.

Twisting Vine Laces

Laces

Repair Value: 29

Entwined

Increases movement speed by 10% per level, but deals 2 damage per level when unequipped.
