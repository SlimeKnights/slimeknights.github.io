Slimecage

The slimecage is the ultimate unarmed combat weapon with its ribcage traits boosting unarmed attacks. Created by pouring slime on a ribcage.

Properties:

  • Low defense
  • 75% Unarmed Attack
  • 3 Upgrade Slots
  • 0 Defense Slots
  • 2 Ability Slots
  • Slime & 2 Ribcage Traits
  • Reach

Slimeshell

The slimeshell improves storage and grants useful unique bonuses. They are created by pouring slime on a shell.

Properties:

  • Low defense
  • 3 Upgrade Slots
  • 0 Defense Slots
  • 2 Ability Slots
  • Slime & Shell Traits
  • Shell Storage
