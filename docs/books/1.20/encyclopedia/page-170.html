Molten Emerald

Molten emerald is the choice for mining when you need silk touch.

Block Effects:

  • Mines up to hardness 10.0 per level with Silk Touch

Molten Diamond

Molten diamond lets you maximize the drops of your ores, but is it worth the cost of the fluid?

Block Effects:

  • Mines up to hardness 10.0 per level with Fortune III
