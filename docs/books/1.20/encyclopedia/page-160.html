Fluid Effects

Fluid effects define the behavior when a fluid hits a block or entity through various modifiers such as spitting, spilling, and splashing.

Water

Water is not the most useful fluid, but can protect you from fire in hot environments.

Entity Effects:

  • 2.0 Water Damage per level

  • Extinguishes fire

Block Effects:

  • Breaks blocks broken by water
