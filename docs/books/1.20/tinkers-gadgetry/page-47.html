Melting Pan

The melting pan will melt your blocks and hold fluid for your staffly goals. It can be found from dungeons, igloos, zombies, and as a gift from villagers.

Properties:

  • Mines meltable blocks

  • Melting+

  • 1 Defense Slot

  • 1 Upgrade Slot

  • 2 Ability Slots

War Pick

The war pick is a combination of pickaxe and a crossbow. It can be found in mineshafts and obtained from villagers or illagers.

Properties:

  • Mines stone blocks and similar

  • Able to keep an arrow prepared to fire

  • Mining blocks boosts velocity of loaded arrow

  • 3 Upgrade Slots

  • 1 Ability Slot
