Slimecage

Become the ultimate boxer by pouring slime on a ribcage in a basin!

Provides many useful melee traits to buff your unarmed attacks.

Properties:

  • Low Armor
  • 0 Defense Slots
  • 3 Upgrade Slots
  • 2 Ability Slots
  • Ideal for Unarmed Melee
  • Reach

Slimeshell

Slimeshells are one part defense, one part storage, granting the best of both shulker abilities.

To create these wondrous pants, pour slime on a shell in a basin.

Properties:

  • Low Armor
  • 0 Defense Slots
  • 3 Upgrade Slots
  • 2 Ability Slots
  • Shell Storage
