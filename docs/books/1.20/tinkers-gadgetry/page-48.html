Battlesign

The battlesign is a combination of a melee weapon and a shield. It will typically be located in bastions, and can be obtained from piglins.

Properties:

  • Medium Damage

  • Medium Block Potential

  • 3 Defense Slots

  • 1 Ability Slot

Swasher

The swasher is a combination of a melee weapon and a handheld fluid pistol found in various pirate locations.

Properties:

  • Medium Damage

  • High Attack Speed

  • Fluid Pistol

  • 3 Upgrade Slots

  • 1 Ability Slot
