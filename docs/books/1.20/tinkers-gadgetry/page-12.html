Knightmetal Shards

Like steel shards, knightmetal shards are primarily obtained from slimes, though in this case its enderslimes with a knightmetal core. Similarly, they can be found in enderslime geodes, again not regrowing after harvesting. These metal shards can also be located in the Twilight Forest, though to many the knowledge of reaching that dimension has become lost.

Skyslime Islands

Skyslime islands can be found around the overworld floating above the terrain or in the ocean. Legend tells that skyslimes were invaders from another realm, though these days they are mostly found near skyslime grass. They are experts in forging steel.
