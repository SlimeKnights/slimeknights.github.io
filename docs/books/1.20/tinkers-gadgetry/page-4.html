Fancy Armor Stands

Leveraging other types of wood and bones, I invented new types of armor stands.

Fancy Armor Stands

  • Clear: Turn invisible when an item is placed on it.

  • Bamboo: Smaller armor stand for compact armor storage.

  • Bone: Has arms, letting it be armed! Interact with the base to place items, and can hold shields in the offhand.

  • Necrotic Bone: Always appears fully lit, even in darkness.
