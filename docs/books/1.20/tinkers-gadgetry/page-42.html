Swasher

The swasher is a combination of a melee weapon and a handheld fluid pistol found in various pirate locations.

Properties:

  • Medium Damage
  • High Attack Speed
  • Fluid Pistol
  • 3 Upgrade Slots
  • 1 Ability Slot

Ancient - General

Head

Durability: 745

Mining Tier: Netherite

Mining Speed: 7

Melee Damage: 2.5

Vintage

Worldbound

Found on war picks, swashers, and battlesigns, plus from bartering. Each level grants +1 ability slot at the cost of -10% attack speed, -10% mining speed, and +10% gravity.
