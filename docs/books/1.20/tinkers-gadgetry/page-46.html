Slimeboots

These boots take the bounciness of slime to enhance your jumping and movement.

To create these fantastic boots, pour slime on laces in a basin.

Properties:

  • Low Armor
  • 0 Defense Slots
  • 3 Upgrade Slots
  • 2 Ability Slots
  • Bouncy

Slimelytra

Fall with style!

To create these futuristic wings, you simply need to pour slime on an elytra in a basin. The slimelytra can later be repaired using phantom membranes in addition to it's slime type.

Properties:

  • Low Armor
  • 0 Defense Slots
  • 4 Upgrade Slots
  • 1 Ability Slot
  • Wings
