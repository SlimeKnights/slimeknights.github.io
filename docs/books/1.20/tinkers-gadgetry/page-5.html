Fluid Cannon

Thruul's fluid cannon is pretty cool, but I find myself limited by the fluid capacity. Combining shulker and knightmetal let me shoot faster and hold more fluid, though not as strong as Nemea's design.

The original design is still probably better for placing blocks since it uses less fluid, but my design can still use tools pretty well.

Staffs

Combining slime crystals and slime wood aids in creation of very flexible tools. Just need a bit of metal to hold the whole thing together.
