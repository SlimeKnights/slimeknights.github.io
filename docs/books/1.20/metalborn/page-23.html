Quartz

Ferring: Trueself

Stores: Identity

Storing Per Metalmind:

  • Allows filling unsealed metalminds

  • Prevents using metalminds with your identity

  • Can use quartz regardless of powers

Tapping Per Metalmind:

  • Allows using metalminds of the stored identity

  • Prevents using metalminds with your identity

Nicrosil

Ferring: Soulbearer

Stores: Investiture

Target: Players

Storing Per Metalmind:

  • Temporarily removes ferring power

  • Metalmind filled with matching power

  • Can use nicrosil regardless of powers

Tapping Per Metalmind:

  • Grants access to stored ferring power
