Soulbound Spikes

While changing your power is safer, soulbound spikes grant truly permanent access to a power as they remain even upon death.

These spikes are forged from an echo shard and two matching spikes: metal filled from monsters and nicrosil from a player.

This process is difficult as it requires stealing power from a player, though you can leverage invested nuggets to steal it from yourself.
