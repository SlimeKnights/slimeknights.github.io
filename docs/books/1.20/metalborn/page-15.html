Tin

Ferring: Windwhisperer

Stores: Sight

Target: Skeletons

Storing Per Metalmind:

  • Grants Blindness

  • Does not scale with multiple metalminds

Tapping Per Metalmind:

  • Grants Night Vision

  • Does not scale with multiple metalminds

Pewter

Ferring: Brute

Stores: Strength

Target: Creepers

Storing Per Metalmind:

  • -0.5 Attack Damage

  • -2 Max Health

Tapping Per Metalmind:

  • +0.5 Attack Damage

  • +2 Max Health

  • Restores extra health immediately

  • Stopping removes the extra health, which may be fatal
