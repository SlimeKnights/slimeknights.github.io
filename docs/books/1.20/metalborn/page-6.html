Rings

Rings are the most basic type of metalmind created in the metal forge. They are capable of storing a small amount of power, typically around 5 minutes, and you can wear up to 8 at once. While they do not store a lot of power, they do make it easier to store or tap a lot of power at once for stronger effects.

You can right click a ring with another ring storing the same type of power to quickly transfer it.

Bracers

Bracers can be created in the metal forge to store large amounts of power, typically around 50 minutes. Due to their size, its only possible to wear 2 of them, but the duration makes up for it.

Like rings, bracers allow transferring power by right clicking. Notably, you can also transfer power between rings and bracers to divide the power in order to tap more metalminds at once.
