Ferrings

Where I come from, being born with any metallic power is rare. It seems in this land though, everyone is born as a ferring with access to a single power. Additionally, resurrection seems to changing their ferring type.

The simplest way to determine your power is by interacting with metal to see if you feel a pulling. Once you find such a metal, you can form it into metalminds and use it to store power for later use.
