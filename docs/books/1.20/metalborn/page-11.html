Creating Spikes

Empty spikes can be created in the metal forge. Different types will grant different powers, but also require killing different types of monsters to fill. Once full, the spike will begin to glow.

As a last resort, it is possible to create a spike from yourself. Using a nicrosil spike will fill it with your ferring power, but your power will be permanently lost.

Spikes can be used in either hand to kill monsters.

Using Spikes

You can use the metalmind UI to insert spikes. Humans can only use up to 4 spikes before the effects become too taxing on the spirit.

Hovering over the "Spike" icon summarizes effects of spikes
