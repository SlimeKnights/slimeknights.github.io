Bane of Sssss

The best upgrade for those with arachnophobia or creeperphobia. Spiders and creepers beware.

Effects:

  • Increases damage and applies slowness to spiders and creepery crawlies

  • Multiple levels, each level increases damage further

  • Requires 1 upgrade slot per level

Cooling

The best way to beat the fires of nether is to freeze them, extinguishing their eternal flame.

Effects:

  • Increases damage to fire based creatures

  • Multiple levels, each level increases damage further

  • Requires 1 upgrade slot per level
