Traveler's Shield

Unfortunately, makes a poor frisbee.

Shields are important for defending yourself against attacks in the world, best to take one with when you go traveling.

Properties:

  • Low Durability

  • Low Block Potential

  • High speed while shielding

  • 2 Upgrade Slots

  • 2 Defense Slots

  • 1 Ability Slot

  • 2 Material Traits

Plate Helmet
