Armor Upgrades

Armor upgrades provide ways to modify your jump, speed, and other such factors on armor and shields.

Blockade

Seal your shield to prevent the damage from entering.

Effects:

  • Increases maximum amount blocked

  • Multiple levels, each further increasing amount

  • Requires 1 upgrade slot per level

  • Requires blocking or parrying
