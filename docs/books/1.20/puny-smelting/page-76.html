Ricochet

Time to make a daring escape.

Effects:

  • Increases knockback you receive

  • Multiple levels, each level further increases knockback

  • Requires 1 upgrade slot per level

Springy

Away your foes go, giving you enough time to turn heels and run, or heal.

Effects:

  • Provides a chance to knockback attackers

  • Multiple levels, each level further increases the chance to knockback

  • Requires 1 upgrade slot per level
