The Melter

This here is your most basic melter able to perform a variety of simple tasks.

Features

  • Early smeltery

  • Gives +3 nuggets or +1 gem per ore

  • Heater can be replaced with seared tank which can increase temperature

  • Internal capacity of 24 ingots

  • Melts 3 items at once

  • Very fuel efficient

  • Ideal for melting piglin drops such as gold swords or armor
