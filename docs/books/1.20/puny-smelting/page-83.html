Depth Strider

This is either madness or brilliance. Its remarkable how often those two traits coincide.

Effects:

  • Increases movement speed underwater

  • Multiple levels, each level further increases speed

  • Requires 1 upgrade slot per level

Feather Falling

Lighter than a feather, but more than twice as deadly.

Effects:

  • Reduces fall damage

  • Multiple levels, each level decreases fall damage further

  • Requires 1 upgrade slot every other level
