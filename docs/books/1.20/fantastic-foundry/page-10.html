Scorched Tanks

Tanks are good for storing molten fluids or fuels. If you want to melt metals in your foundry you'll need one! Tanks cannot be used for the floor of the foundry.

Tanks can be filled from buckets, potions, and can also be poured into from faucets. They can also be used to cast without a foundry. Pistons break them, and dispensers can place them.

Scorched Blocks

These blocks can be used for any part of the foundry, as floors or walls. They are also useful for decorating your base.
